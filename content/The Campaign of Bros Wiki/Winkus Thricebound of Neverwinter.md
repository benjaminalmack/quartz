A gnome bard with untamed ginger curls, a lute shaped like a pineapple, and the attention span of a raccoon on sugar. He's on a "legend hunt" for stories too absurd to be true—until he sees one of your adventurers with a third leg. His muse strikes instantly.

---

Armor Class 13 (leather armor)  
Hit Points 22 (5d6 + 5)  
Speed 25 ft.

STR 8 (-1)  
DEX 14 (+2)  
CON 12 (+1)  
INT 10 (+0)  
WIS 11 (+0)  
CHA 17 (+3)

Saving Throws Dex +4, Cha +5  
Skills Performance +7, Insight +2, Persuasion +5  
Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10  
Languages Common, Gnomish, Elvish  
Challenge 1/4 (50 XP)  
Proficiency Bonus +2

  

Inventory:

100cp

---

🎶 Bardic Inspiration (3/day): As a bonus action, Pickle can choose one creature within 60 feet who can hear him, granting a d6 inspiration die.

🎸 Spellcasting: Charisma is his spellcasting ability (save DC 13, +5 to hit). He knows the following:

Cantrips (at will): Vicious Mockery, Prestidigitation, Minor Illusion  
1st level (2 slots): Charm Person, Cure Wounds, Dissonant Whispers

🎤 Musical Quirk: Once per day, Pickle can compose an impromptu verse about something odd, granting advantage on Charisma checks to one creature for the next hour as the rumor spreads.**
